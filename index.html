Quality Protocol

AQL 1.5 / 2.5 Quality Assurance Protocol

Quality is inspected at each stage from raw fabric arrival to pre-shipment container sealing.

Zero-Defect Commitment

Every buyer shipment is audited under international AQL 1.5 / 2.5 criteria. Our quality control team operates independently of production targets to safeguard garment integrity.

4-Point System

100% incoming fabric rolls checked for weaving faults and color shading.

In-Line QC

Real-time traffic-light audit per workstation to catch stitch flaws instantly.

In-House Lab

Shrinkage testing, color fastness to wash/rubbing, and button pull-tests.

Pre-Shipment Audit

Final random inspection (FRI) before container packing.
